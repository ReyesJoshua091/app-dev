# Interstellar

---

**Interstellar** is a 2014 epic science fiction film directed by *Christopher Nolan*. ~~The Earth is fine.~~ Earth is suffering from catastrophic crop blights, and humanity is on the brink of extinction. 

A dedicated group of astronauts must travel through a newly discovered wormhole near Saturn to find a habitable new home.

## Key Themes
- Time dilation and relativity
- Human survival and sacrifice
- The enduring power of connection
